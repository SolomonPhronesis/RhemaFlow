Two useful research directions

Source-backed native inference

NVIDIA’s official NeMo-Speech.cpp documents Apple Silicon/Metal support. Its cache-aware Nemotron English route and Sortformer diarization are candidates for an approved experiment. The inspected TDT v3 route is full-utterance only.

Pinned runtime model capabilities

Conditional weight adaptation

The local Qwen header implies about 28.17 GB of conventional full Adam state before activations. Decoder attention LoRA would add 3.21M parameters at rank 8 or 6.42M at rank 16. Compatible training and peak memory remain unverified.

No R2T2 weights, logits, transcripts or teacher outputs are used. Bark is text-to-audio, not an ASR candidate.

Smallest valid next experiment

  1. Reach the 8 GiB pilot floor, aiming for 15 GiB for heavy work, without unapproved cleanup.
  2. Verify consent, rights, hashes and an approved compatible local runtime.
  3. Run the four priority models one at a time on paired clean/MP3 target clips and an independent target-absent music/chatter control.
  4. Preserve actual transcripts, correct/wrong cards, cold/warm time, end-to-end p50/p95, RSS, energy and dropouts.
  5. Only then expand to matched noisy-target conditions and frozen real church gold. At least three services and independent transcripts are prerequisites, not proof of accuracy.

Local tests now reject evidence-class confusion, train/holdout contamination, unmatched audio, missing card observations and false retention. They do not classify a speaker or change any product.

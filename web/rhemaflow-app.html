Voice commands

Say any of these. Short phrases only work in short sentences so normal preaching never triggers them. A scripture reference in the same sentence wins over navigation. Close variants and run-together words ("netverse") are understood.

Help

Keys (when not typing): Space approve first card / next in plan · X dismiss · 1-5 pick option · ← → or P/N verse · ↑ ↓ chapter · B blackout · C clear · R read aloud · S stop reading · F stage window · / search · H help.

Voice (say it naturally): "John chapter 3 verse 16", "next verse", "previous chapter", "zoom 300", "max zoom", "half max zoom", "blackout", "clear the screen", "approve it", "option two", "read it", "stop reading", "open stage", "close stage", "auto approve on".

Browser limits (honest): speech recognition uses the browser's own service (Chrome/Edge send audio to the vendor's servers; Safari 14.1+ works, Firefox has none). Browsers only allow full screen and opening a window after a click, so "full screen" and "open stage" by voice may be refused: click once in the stage window and press F. "Exit full screen" by voice works. For a fully offline, more accurate setup use the desktop app.
